Ohren-Übung — sources and credits

Music: Johann Sebastian Bach (1685–1750), public domain. Markings, captions and definitions are our own.

Scores the notes were read from
- Goldberg Variations canons and The Art of Fugue (Contrapunctus I, VI): The Mutopia Project,
  https://www.mutopiaproject.org — Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 or public domain, per piece.
  The note data derived from them is shared under the same licence.
- Well-Tempered Clavier I fugues: Humdrum encodings by David Huron, revised by Craig Stuart Sapp,
  https://github.com/humdrum-tools/bach-wtc-fugues
- Inventions and Sinfonias: Humdrum encodings by David Huron, https://github.com/humdrum-tools/inventions
- The Art of Fugue (all other pieces): Humdrum encodings by Craig Stuart Sapp,
  https://github.com/craigsapp/art-of-the-fugue
- Musical Offering, Ricercar a 3 and a 6: Humdrum encodings by Craig Stuart Sapp after the Bach-Gesellschaft
  edition, https://github.com/craigsapp/bach-musical-offering
- Musical Offering canons: transcribed from the Bach-Gesellschaft edition (IMSLP #10042) and its resolutions,
  cross-checked against Reccmo's MIDI files on IMSLP and, for the Canon a 4, the MIDI on dardel.info.
